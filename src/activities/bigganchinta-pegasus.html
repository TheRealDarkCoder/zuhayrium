In 12th grade, published "Pegasus, Zero-Days, and the Story of Spyware" (Bengali) in Bigganchinta following the July 2021 NSO Group disclosures—explaining memory allocation, buffer overflows, OS sandboxing and memory randomization, and the three-bug iOS Trident exploit chain for a general science audience.
