--- title: "Higher Secondary Certificate at Notre Dame College, Dhaka" date: "2020" end: "2022" ---

Completed Bangladesh's national high school qualification (grades 11–12, Science track: physics, chemistry, higher mathematics, and ICT) at Notre Dame College, a selective Holy Cross institution founded in 1949 and one of the few in the country with its own independent entrance exams. Straddling COVID-19 lockdowns and the return to campus, those two years meant starting remotely and staying self-directed before jumping back into full in-person labs, weekly quizzes, and campus life full of short but memorable memories.
