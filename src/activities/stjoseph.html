--- title: "Schooling at St. Joseph Higher Secondary School, Dhaka" date: "2012" end: "2020" ---

Spent eight formative years at St. Joseph Higher Secondary School, a Holy Cross institution in Dhaka founded in 1954 and known for its academics, club culture, and lifelong "Josephite" community, completing Bangladesh's national Secondary School Certificate (SSC, grades 9–10, Science track in English). Longer than I have spent in any other institution, it is where I first started writing, building, and competing—the place that forged who I am, and what I will always consider my home ground.
